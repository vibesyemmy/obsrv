# 0.44.0 driven against five more live sites

The third run of the day, on a stack that is 0.44.0 end to end (installed
app, the session's MCP server, the plugin's skill text), against a set chosen
for what the first two runs had not touched: reddit.com (an infinite feed),
amazon.com (a DOM past the report caps, and a different page under a mobile
UA), nytimes.com (lazy images under sticky chrome, ads that never stop
loading), vercel.com (dark backgrounds, thin type, fractional-density laptop
presets) and notion.so (100vh sections and animation for the banded full
page). Headless, live, and every surface: snap, full page, lint, audit,
report, presets, inspect, diff, throttle, a live drive at 1.5x. The site is
the fixture; this reports on the tool.

## Held

- **Both 0.44.0 fixes on real pages, through the MCP schema.** reddit.com
  under 3G at the default budget: `settled: false`, `unsettledReason:
  "loading"`, `settledMs: null`, the warning naming the throttle and
  `timeoutMs`, PNG inline. nytimes.com never finishes loading headless within
  30 s even unthrottled (ads): three CLI full pages captured 12 bands each
  as a cut load, where 0.43.0 errored. Walks: reddit 21 screenfuls, nytimes
  13 and 15, amazon 12, vercel 11 and 7 — all to the end, none capped.
- **Fractional density.** vercel.com at `laptop-1080-125` headless and
  `laptop-1080-150` live: CSS 1536×864 and 1280×720, the live raster
  1920×1080 device px, srcset density-corrected (a 2784 px candidate drawn at
  1222 device px reads oversized, nothing reads upscaled), contrast grouped
  on dark backgrounds.
- **Caps stay honest.** amazon.com on the phone: 1,046 targets, 200 listed,
  `truncated: 836`, four groups. nytimes.com's full page at 15,500 px: 12
  bands of 768 and the warning that the rest is not in the raster; the report
  counts 6 findings `belowCapture`.
- **Banded full page.** notion.so: 6 bands, the sticky nav hidden after the
  first, `animating` said once.
- **The rest.** inspect on a nytimes headline (16 px, 4.04 mm, 16.2:1);
  diff on notion.so says its two captures are different frames and declines
  to interpret the band deltas; the queue held five and six calls at once with
  the later ones saying how long they waited.

## Broke or misled, ranked

1. **The stuck-chrome probe threw once on nytimes.com, and the warning
   quoted Electron.** The report's full-page render said "could not measure
   chrome stuck to the viewport, so the bands keep it: Script failed to
   execute, this normally means an error was thrown. Check the developer
   tools console." — so the sticky header stayed in every band. Three more
   full pages of the same site (the report again, the CLI twice) measured
   without error and found no stuck bar. The probe's controller lives on the
   page as `window.__obsrvChrome` between four round trips; a page that
   re-navigates or replaces its document between `mark` and `settle` (ads,
   consent, a region redirect) leaves the second call with nothing to call.
   Two things to do: have the probe catch its own failure and say what was
   missing, instead of Electron's generic line; and reinstall the controller
   once when it is gone, since the page is still there to measure.

Observations, not bugs: nytimes.com is a different page run to run — 254
targets and 76 invisible text elements in one report, 83 and 0 in the next —
which is the site's A/B and ad state, not the tool; the 12-band cap on the
full-page capture is by design and documented, distinct from the walk's cap
that 0.44.0 removed.

## Afterwards: three once-seen items, and the fourth investigated

On `fix/once-seen`, off 0.44.0, tests first, 78 e2e green:

- **Any target with no text is named by its icon** (b35bf29). The title,
  label or alt fallback covered icon-only inline anchors only; HN's upvote on
  the phone layout, a block anchor, was "".
- **The stuck-chrome probe survives a page that replaces its document**
  (33b41c2). Every controller call is guarded on the page and answers a
  value, "gone", or the page's own error; a controller that is gone is
  reinstalled once and the probe marks again; gone twice, the warning says
  so. The driving moved into `cli/stuckProbe.ts` with the page access
  injected, unit-tested against a scripted page. nytimes.com ran clean.
- **A drive that flips the preset waits for the page** (22d22c9), the same
  wait the snap has had since 0.43.0, as `settlePage` with its status source
  injected. On bbc.com two flips outlasted the 5 s budget and said so with
  `loading: true`, where the drive used to answer the transitional status
  silently.

**The stale first capture, investigated and not fixed.** Five attempts to
reproduce — two navigate-and-flip calls on the installed app, three fresh
launches of the dev app with the exact first-call sequence (show one page,
then navigate and flip to a phone preset with a pane capture) — all
answered a phone-sized PNG matching the status. The capture path reads:
hold painting, wait for the viewport, `settleTarget` (a fresh frame from the
target, then quiet), `drawNow` to the renderer and its acknowledgement,
`capturePage`. Frames reach the renderer by `webContents.send` on the same
ordered channel as `drawNow`, and the renderer uploads a frame in its
handler before any paint, so the draw request cannot overtake the frame that
settled the capture. What is left is on the renderer's side of the first
call after a launch: its frame handler drops frames while the tab's mode is
not `url` in its store, and delivery opens only once the pane has
subscribed. Either would leave the previous texture on the canvas while
main, which saw its own frame events, called the capture settled. The
diagnostic that would name a recurrence: the renderer's acknowledgement
carries the identity of the frame it painted, and main warns when that is
not the frame it last emitted for the tab.
