# 0.43.0 driven against five more live sites

The same day as the 0.42.0 run and its seven fixes, the released 0.43.0
(installed app, npm package, the session's MCP server, plugin cache) against
a new set, chosen for what each breaks: stripe.com (animating hero, thin
type, 15,000 px), a GitHub repo page (dense small controls, 14,000 px on a
phone), bbc.com (lazy images with low-res placeholders, srcset at 3x, 18,700
px), excalidraw.com (a canvas app shell with nothing to scroll) and a Tailwind
docs page (a sidebar that scrolls on its own). Headless, live, and the
surfaces the first run left out: inspect, diff, throttle, text scale, a
drive with scroll, highlight and capture. The site under test is the fixture;
this reports on the tool.

## Held

- **The walk** ran on every headless audit, lint and report, and live; every
  page over twelve screenfuls said so (`atEnd: false` and the note). Live and
  headless agree on stripe.com: contrast 37 and 37, oversized 2 and 2, thin
  text 226 and 233, and panel findings only where a panel profile was named.
- **Groups only** left the list out and said nothing about it (stripe:
  `truncated.findings: 161`, `warnings` without the sentence).
- **The queue** took five calls at once and again six: the ones that waited
  said so (2.5 s, 16.4 s behind a throttled load), all passed.
- **An app shell with nothing to scroll** (excalidraw): one band, and the
  warning names the reason — overflow hidden, no scrollable container in the
  light DOM, content in a canvas or iframe is past this screen.
- **A sidebar that scrolls on its own** (Tailwind docs): 181 of 208 findings
  classed `inPanel`, pinned nowhere, counted.
- **inspect** on a bbc.com headline at 3x on Budget TN: 24 px = 3.96 mm,
  15.96:1 as stated, 9.12:1 on the panel. **diff** on the GitHub page:
  settled, ink delta −0.15 %, row ratio 0.5, no findings. **text scale** 1.5
  on the Tailwind docs: 31 targets, none under, where ×1 had 235 and 208 —
  the sidebar collapses at 911 CSS px, which is what reflow means.
- **Live drive**: a new tab, a raster capture that says `animating`, a scroll
  to a page-space rect with a highlight drawn at the pane rect it reported.

## Broke or misled, ranked

1. **Past the walk cap, placeholders are findings again.** bbc.com at
   iphone-61 is 18,727 px; the walk stops at twelve screenfuls (10,224 px).
   The lint then reports 41 upscaled images, the exemplar a 160×90 px
   placeholder drawn over 1,083×609 device px at y = 15,843 — below the walk.
   Without the walk it reports 62, so the walk did its work where it went;
   the tail is the 0.42.0 problem for any page taller than twelve screenfuls
   of the preset. The cap is the full-page capture's (`MAX_TILE_BANDS`); the
   walk costs 150 ms a screenful, so the whole of bbc.com is about 3 s.
   Either the walk's cap goes (its 15 s budget already bounds it), or the
   lint says how many images sat past the walked height.
   **Fixed on `fix/walk-cap`:** both. The cap is gone from both walks, the
   budget is the only bound, and a lint after a walk that ran out of budget
   counts the image findings below the height it reached. bbc.com now walks
   19 screenfuls to the end in 5.0 s and reports 32 upscaled: 23 are srcset
   files of 320 or 640 px drawn larger at 3x, the rule's business; 9 are grey
   placeholders four abreast at two heights, a carousel's offscreen slides,
   which no vertical walk reaches.
2. **Under a throttle, a slow load is a bare error.** `obsrv_snap` bbc.com at
   android-65 with `budget-phone`: "obsrv snap failed (exit 1): obsrv: load
   did not finish within 30000 ms". With `timeoutMs: 90000` the same call
   settles at `settledMs: 69585`. The error names neither the throttle in
   force nor `timeoutMs`; nothing is captured though the page had painted
   (the 70 s render shows it), and the tool text promises "only a render that
   painted nothing errors". Under a throttle the load budget is the
   measurement running out, not a failure: capture what painted, `settled:
   false`, `settledMs: null`, and a warning naming the throttle and the
   budget.
   **Fixed on `fix/walk-cap`:** a render captures the page as it stands
   after a cut load, `settled: false` with a new `unsettledReason:
   "loading"`, `settledMs` null, the warning naming the throttle and
   `--timeout`; the measurements still error, with the same sentence. The
   first cut read the capture's own verdict and said settled with
   `settledMs: 30413` beside the warning — a paint-quiet frame is not a
   settled page — so the render sets the reason itself.
3. **`tabs` went from six to one between two drive calls** — resolved: the
   person at the window had closed them. The answer was right.

Observation, not a bug: bbc.com's srcset labels a 1,024 px file `160w`, and
the lint reports it as "1024×576 px (the 160w candidate)" — true to the data,
and the reader can see the descriptor lies.

One more thing seen once during this: `obsrv_drive { preset: "iphone-61" }`
on the bbc.com tab, right after a fresh navigation, answered `url:
"about:blank"`, `loading: false` while `tabs` still said bbc.com; a status
read moments later had the page. The app's `setPreset` confirms once the
recreated target has a page or is loading, and the drive reads the status
straight after — so a reload that outlasts the apply budget without ever
reading `loading: true` hands the drive the blank beat. Two later flips (to
android-65, back to 1080p-24) answered the page at once. The snap path
settles and re-reads after its capture since 0.43.0; the drive path does
not. Not fixed here.
